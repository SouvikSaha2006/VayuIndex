Statistical formulation, traffic weighting schemas, and price relative methodology augmenting the official Indian Consumer Price Index (CPI) transport inflation subgroup.

1. Mathematical Formulation (Laspeyres Price Index)

VayuIndex computes the daily airfare price index using the standard Laspeyres Price Index formula weighted by fixed base-period passenger traffic weights (Wi) across top Indian domestic trunk corridors:

DAILY AIRFARE LASPEYRES INDEX FORMULA
It = [ ∑(Pi,t · Wi) / ∑(Pi,0 · Wi) ] × 100
It Computed Laspeyres Airfare Index value for day t (Base = 100.00).
Pi,t Median observed one-way fare for trunk route corridor i on date t.
Pi,0 Calibrated base benchmark baseline fare for trunk route corridor i in INR.
Wi Passenger traffic weight assigned to route i derived from DGCA annual seat capacity distribution.

2. Why Airfares Matter for National CPI Inflation Tracking

In the official Consumer Price Index (CPI) published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MOSPI), transport and communication represent a significant weight in urban household expenditure.

📈

High Fare Volatility

Dynamic airline yield management algorithms cause fares to fluctuate up to 300% close to departure dates.

⚡

Real-time Signal vs Lagged Data

Traditional CPI surveys lag by 30-45 days. VayuIndex provides daily high-frequency real-time inflation signals.

🎯

Basket Weight Calibration

Fixed base weights prevent distorted spikes from low-volume regional routes while giving true weight to heavy metro trunks.
